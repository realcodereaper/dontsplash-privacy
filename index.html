Privacy Policy

Don't Splash: Poolrooms Monkeys, by Code Reaper Games
Effective date: November 20, 2026

The short version
  • We don't sell your data and there are no ads or ad tracking.
  • Your Meta display name is shown to other players in online games.
  • Voice chat is live only. It is not recorded, and you can turn it off at any time.
  • Your progress is backed up to your Meta account so you don't lose it.
  • Purchases are cosmetic only and are handled by Meta. We never see your payment details.

We're Code Reaper Games, the small studio that makes Don't Splash: Poolrooms Monkeys for Meta Quest. This page explains what information the game uses, why it needs it, and the choices you have.

1. Information the game uses

From your Meta account (through Meta's platform services):

  • Your Meta user ID and display name, so other players can see who they are playing with, and so friends can invite and join you.
  • Your age group (for example, whether the account belongs to a 10–12 year old). We use this only to start voice chat switched off for younger players. We do not receive your date of birth.
  • Your list of blocked users, so people you've blocked are muted and hidden for you.
  • Proof that you own the game (an entitlement check required by the store).
  • Which cosmetic items you have bought, so they unlock again if you reinstall.

Game progress: bananas, levels, unlocked skins and hats, badges, challenge progress, your monkey's colour, and your comfort and accessibility settings. These are saved on your headset and backed up to your Meta account (Meta's user data store) so they survive a reinstall or a new headset.

Leaderboards and achievements: your best times and earned badges are sent to Meta's leaderboard and achievement services and may be shown to other players with your display name.

Online play: to connect you to other players, the game uses Unity Gaming Services (Relay and Lobby). Unity creates an anonymous player ID for your device. Lobby information such as the room code, the number of players and the game mode is shared with the players in that lobby. While you play, your monkey's position, movements, hand poses and chosen cosmetics are sent to the other players in the same game.

Voice chat: in online games, proximity voice chat is provided by Unity Vivox. Your voice is sent live to the players near you and is not recorded or stored by us. Solo games have no voice chat. The game also measures how loud you are speaking, on your headset only, to animate your monkey's mouth. That loudness value is not recorded or sent anywhere.

Things that stay on your headset: photos from the in-game photo booth and the lobby mirror stay in the game and are not uploaded. Your settings are also stored locally.

2. What we don't do

  • We don't show ads, and we don't use advertising or cross-app tracking.
  • We don't sell or rent personal information to anyone.
  • We don't collect your real name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, or photos from your device.
  • We don't record voice chat.
  • We don't use your camera or your room scan data. The game only uses the play area the headset already provides.

3. Purchases

The game is free to play. Optional purchases are cosmetic items only (such as skins) and never give a gameplay advantage. The daily banana box is free and is never sold. All payments are processed by Meta. We receive only a record that an item was bought, never your card or payment details. Parents can manage purchase settings through the Meta Horizon app's parental supervision tools.

4. Children and teens

Meta Quest accounts are available to people aged 10 and over, and accounts for children aged 10–12 are managed by a parent. We designed the game with younger players in mind:

  • Voice chat starts off for accounts aged 10–12. A player or parent can choose to turn it on.
  • Anyone can switch their own microphone off, mute everyone else, or mute, block or report a specific player from inside the game.
  • The headset's Report button uses Meta's standard reporting flow.
  • There is no free-text chat in the game.
  • We don't knowingly collect more personal information from a child than is needed to play.

Our community Discord is linked from the store page for bug reports. Discord requires users to be 13 or older and has its own privacy policy. The game itself doesn't link to or share any data with Discord.

5. Who we share information with

We share information only with the services needed to run the game:

  • Meta Platforms: account, entitlement, purchases, cloud saves, leaderboards, achievements, friends, invites, blocking and reporting. See the Meta Quest privacy policy.
  • Unity Technologies: online matchmaking and connections (Relay and Lobby) and voice chat (Vivox). Unity may also receive basic technical information such as the device model and performance data. See the Unity game player privacy policy.
  • Other players: your display name, your avatar and its movements, your cosmetics, your voice while voice chat is on, and your leaderboard times.
  • When required by law, or to protect players' safety, for example in response to a report of abuse.

6. How long information is kept

  • Progress on your headset is kept until you uninstall the game or clear its data.
  • Progress backed up to your Meta account is kept until you ask us to delete it or delete your Meta account.
  • Voice chat is not stored.
  • Leaderboard entries may be reset from time to time, for example at the end of a season.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Turn voice chat, your microphone, or hearing other players on or off in the game's lobby and wrist menu.
  • Mute, block or report any player.
  • Play Solo, which doesn't connect to other players.
  • Ask us to delete your cloud-saved progress, or ask what information we hold about you, using the contact details below. A parent can make these requests for their child.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA or COPPA), you may have extra rights to access, correct or delete your information, or to object to how it is used. We will honour these requests.

8. Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections through Meta's and Unity's services. No system is perfectly secure, but we only use the information needed to run the game, which keeps the risk low.

9. Changes to this policy

The game is in Early Access and will change as it grows. If we change how information is used, we will update this page and its effective date, and we will post major changes in our Discord announcements.
