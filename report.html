Report a Player

Don't Splash: Poolrooms Monkeys, by Code Reaper Games

Is someone in danger right now? Contact your local emergency services first. If you're a young player, tell a parent or another adult you trust about anything that upset you.

Fastest: report from inside the game

  • Block a player on the PLAYERS board in the lobby. You won't see or hear them again.
  • Mute one player, or everyone, from the same board or the wrist menu.
  • Use the Report button on your Quest (press the Meta button, then Report). This sends the report to Meta, with a recording of what happened, so their safety team can act on the person's account.

Or send a report to us

Fill this in and press the button. It opens an email to us, already written, so you just press send. We read every report.

If no email opened, send your report to codereaper.support@gmail.com with the player's name, what happened and when.

What happens next

  • We look at every report, usually within a few days.
  • Players who break the rules can be removed from rooms and banned from the game.
  • Please don't share passwords, addresses or other private details in your report. We only use what you send to look into the problem. See our privacy policy.
